Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) 
Version 1.0 
 
Account Management FAQs 
Q1. How do I create an account? 
A: 
Visit the registration page and complete the signup process using your company information. 
 
Q2. How do I update company information? 
A: 
Navigate to Account Settings and update the required company details. 
 
Q3. How do I view account details? 
A: 
Go to Account Settings to view company information, subscription details, and account status. 
 
Q4. How do I check account status? 
A: 
Account status is available in the Account Overview section. 
Possible statuses include: 
• 
Active 
• 
Trial 
• 
Suspended 
• 
Cancelled 
 
Q5. How do I change account ownership? 
A: 
Only the current account owner can transfer ownership to another administrator. 
 
Q6. What happens when an account is suspended? 
A: 
Users may lose access to certain services until the issue is resolved. 
 
Q7. Can multiple administrators manage the same account? 
A: 
Yes. Multiple administrators can be assigned to an account. 
 
Q8. How do I reactivate an account? 
A: 
Contact support or resolve any outstanding billing issues. 
 
User Management FAQs 
Q9. How do I add a user? 
A: 
Navigate to User Management and select Add User. 
 
Q10. How do I remove a user? 
A: 
Select the user and click Remove User. 
 
Q11. How do I edit a user role? 
A: 
Open User Management, select the user, and update their assigned role. 
 
Q12. What user roles are available? 
A: 
Available roles include: 
• 
Owner 
• 
Admin 
• 
Manager 
• 
Member 
 
Q13. How many users can I add? 
A: 
The number of users depends on your available seat count. 
 
Q14. Why can't I add another user? 
A: 
You may have reached the maximum number of purchased seats. 
 
Q15. Can I temporarily disable a user? 
A: 
Yes. Users may be deactivated without being permanently removed. 
 
Subscription FAQs 
Q16. How do I change my subscription plan? 
A: 
Navigate to Billing → Subscription → Change Plan. 
 
Q17. When does an upgrade take effect? 
A: 
Plan upgrades take effect immediately. 
 
Q18. When does a downgrade take effect? 
A: 
Downgrades become active during the next billing cycle. 
 
Q19. What subscription plans are available? 
A: 
Available plans include: 
• 
Starter 
• 
Professional 
• 
Business 
• 
Enterprise 
 
Q20. Can I switch from monthly to annual billing? 
A: 
Yes. Billing frequency can be modified from Subscription Settings. 
 
Q21. Will my features change after upgrading? 
A: 
Yes. Additional features and limits may become available. 
 
Billing FAQs 
Q22. Where can I view invoices? 
A: 
Navigate to Billing → Invoices. 
 
Q23. How do I download an invoice? 
A: 
Select the invoice and click Download. 
 
Q24. Why was I charged this month? 
A: 
Charges are generated based on active subscriptions and seat usage. 
 
Q25. What payment methods are supported? 
A: 
Supported methods include: 
• 
Credit Cards 
• 
Debit Cards 
• 
ACH Transfers 
• 
Wire Transfers 
 
Q26. Why did my payment fail? 
A: 
Common reasons include: 
• 
Insufficient funds 
• 
Expired card 
• 
Bank restrictions 
 
Q27. How do I update my payment method? 
A: 
Navigate to Billing Settings and add a new payment method. 
 
Q28. What happens if I miss a payment? 
A: 
Your account may become restricted until payment is received. 
 
Q29. How do I view billing history? 
A: 
Billing history is available under Billing Reports. 
 
Q30. Can I receive invoices by email? 
A: 
Yes. Invoice email notifications can be enabled in settings. 
 
Seat Management FAQs 
Q31. What is a seat? 
A: 
A seat represents one active user within the platform. 
 
Q32. How do I purchase additional seats? 
A: 
Navigate to Seat Management and select Add Seats. 
 
Q33. Are additional seats billed immediately? 
A: 
Yes. Additional seats are billed on a prorated basis. 
 
Q34. Can unused seats be refunded? 
A: 
Unused seats are generally non-refundable. 
 
Q35. How do I reduce seats? 
A: 
Seat reductions can be scheduled for the next billing cycle.

Refund FAQs 
Q52. How do I request a refund? 
A: 
Submit a refund request through Billing Support. 
 
Q53. How long do refunds take? 
A: 
Approved refunds are typically processed within 5–10 business days. 
 
Q54. Can I request a refund after 30 days? 
A: 
Refund requests submitted after 30 days are generally not eligible. 
 
Q55. Can partial refunds be issued? 
A: 
Partial refunds may be reviewed on a case-by-case basis. 
 
Cancellation FAQs 
Q56. How do I cancel my subscription? 
A: 
Navigate to Billing → Subscription → Cancel Subscription. 
 
Q57. Does cancellation happen immediately? 
A: 
No. Cancellation becomes effective at the end of the current billing cycle. 
 
Q58. Will I lose access immediately after cancellation? 
A: 
No. Access remains available until the current billing period ends. 
 
Q59. Can I reactivate a cancelled subscription? 
A: 
Yes. Contact support or reactivate through Subscription Settings. 
 
Q60. What happens to my data after cancellation? 
A: 
Data enters the retention period according to company policy.
