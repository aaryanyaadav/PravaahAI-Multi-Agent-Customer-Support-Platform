1. Account Policy 
An account represents an organization subscribed to Nexus Support AI. 
Each account contains: 
• 
Company information 
• 
Users 
• 
Subscription details 
• 
Billing records 
• 
Support history 
Accounts must maintain accurate information at all times. 
Account ownership belongs to the subscribing organization. 
Nexus Support AI reserves the right to suspend accounts that violate company policies. 
Inactive accounts may be archived after prolonged inactivity. 
 
2. User Management Policy 
Users may be assigned one of the following roles: 
• 
Owner 
• 
Admin 
• 
Manager 
• 
Member 
Only Owners and Admins may: 
• 
Add users 
• 
Remove users 
• 
Change subscription settings 
• 
Access billing information 
Organizations are responsible for managing user permissions. 
Unauthorized sharing of credentials is prohibited. 
 
3. Subscription Policy 
Nexus Support AI offers the following subscription tiers: 
• 
Starter 
• 
Professional 
• 
Business 
• 
Enterprise 
Subscriptions are billed according to the selected billing cycle. 
Supported billing cycles include: 
• 
Monthly 
• 
Quarterly 
• 
Annual 
Subscription upgrades become effective immediately. 
Subscription downgrades become effective at the start of the next billing cycle. 
Subscription changes may impact available features and usage limits. 
 
4. Billing Policy 
Invoices are automatically generated according to the active subscription. 
Customers are responsible for maintaining valid payment methods. 
Supported payment methods include: 
• 
Credit Cards 
• 
Debit Cards 
• 
ACH Transfers 
• 
Wire Transfers 
Invoices must be paid by the due date indicated on the invoice. 
Failure to pay invoices may result in service restrictions or account suspension. 
Billing disputes must be submitted within thirty (30) days of the invoice date. 
5. Refund Policy 
Refund requests may be submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of the original payment 
date. 
Refunds are only applicable to successfully paid invoices. 
Refund requests submitted after thirty (30) days are not eligible. 
Refund eligibility may depend on: 
• 
Subscription status 
• 
Payment verification 
• 
Account standing 
Approved refunds are processed within five to ten business days. 
Refunds are returned to the original payment method whenever possible. 
Partial refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. 
Enterprise agreements may contain custom refund terms. 
 
6. Cancellation Policy 
Customers may cancel subscriptions at any time. 
Cancellation requests do not immediately terminate service. 
The subscription remains active until the end of the current billing cycle. 
No prorated refunds are provided for unused portions of the billing cycle unless otherwise 
specified in a contractual agreement. 
Cancelled accounts may continue to access paid services until the expiration date of the current 
billing period. 
 
7. Seat Management Policy 
Each subscription includes a defined number of seats. 
A seat represents one active user. 
Organizations may purchase additional seats at any time. 
Additional seats are billed on a prorated basis. 
Unused seats do not generate refunds. 
Seat reductions take effect during the next billing cycle. 
Seat availability depends on the active subscription plan. 
 
8. Service Level Agreement (SLA) 
Nexus Support AI provides the following support response targets. 
Critical Priority Tickets: 
• 
Initial Response Time: 1 Hour 
High Priority Tickets: 
• 
Initial Response Time: 4 Hours 
Medium Priority Tickets: 
• 
Initial Response Time: 1 Business Day 
Low Priority Tickets: 
• 
Initial Response Time: 3 Business Days 
SLA targets are goals and not guaranteed resolution times. 
Enterprise customers may have custom SLA agreements. 
 
9. Support Ticket Policy 
Customers may submit support requests through: 
• 
Support Portal 
• 
Email Support 
• 
Enterprise Support Channels 
Each ticket receives: 
• 
Ticket ID 
• 
Priority 
• 
Status 
• 
Assigned Team 
Ticket priorities include: 
• 
Low 
• 
Medium 
• 
High 
• 
Critical 
Customers should provide accurate descriptions to facilitate faster resolution. 
Duplicate tickets may be merged. 
Resolved tickets may be reopened within fourteen (14) days of closure. 
 
10. Escalation Policy 
Tickets may be escalated under the following circumstances: 
• 
Critical business impact 
• 
Security incidents 
• 
Extended resolution delays 
• 
Enterprise customer requests 
Escalated tickets receive additional review by senior support personnel. 
Escalation does not guarantee immediate resolution. 
Human review may be required for complex cases. 
 
11. Data Retention Policy 
Customer records are retained for seven (7) years. 
Audit logs are retained for three (3) years. 
Deleted accounts enter an archival state for ninety (90) days. 
Archived data may be permanently removed after the retention period expires. 
Legal and regulatory obligations may require extended retention. 
 
12. Security Policy 
Nexus Support AI employs multiple security controls including: 
• 
AES-256 encryption at rest 
• 
TLS 1.3 encryption in transit 
• 
Multi-factor authentication 
• 
Role-based access control 
• 
Audit logging 
Security incidents are investigated according to internal response procedures. 
Customers are responsible for safeguarding credentials. 
 
13. Privacy Policy 
Customer information is processed only for legitimate business purposes. 
Personal information is handled according to applicable privacy regulations. 
Customer data is never sold to third parties. 
Access to customer information is restricted to authorized personnel. 
Customers may request information regarding stored personal data. 
 
14. Acceptable Use Policy 
Customers may not use Nexus Support AI for: 
• 
Illegal activities 
• 
Fraudulent activities 
• 
Distribution of malware 
• 
Unauthorized system access 
• 
Abuse of platform resources 
Violation of acceptable use policies may result in suspension or termination of services.

Dedicated account management 
•

• 
Custom SLAs 
• 
Custom billing arrangements 
•

Updated policies become effective upon publication unless otherwise specified. 
Customers are encouraged to periodically review policy updates.
